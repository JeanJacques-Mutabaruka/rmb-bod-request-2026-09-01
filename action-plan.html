Legal stocktake of existing reporting obligations

What this activity consists in

Scope is deliberately limited to mineral trading companies for this first cycle. WS3   Quantification of losses to the Rwandan economy   (08 Sep 2026 to 15 Nov 2026, 69 days (~2 months))
A3.1
Definition of the loss taxonomy, historical period and data sources
  • Define each of the four loss categories precisely, with its calculation formula and the evidence that supports or contests it
  • Propose the historical period to be covered (a number of past years) for Senior Management to confirm
  • Identify the data sources for each category: signed contracts, invoices, assay reports and prefinancing agreements already held by RMB
 Loss taxonomy, methodology note and confirmed historical period Mineral market intelligence expert Legal (P), extraction and inspection division 08 Sep 2026 (D+7) 15 Oct 2026 (D+44) 38 days (~1 month) none
What this activity consists in

Before any number is produced, the four categories the Board named need a precise, defensible definition, because 'losses' is otherwise a word anyone can dispute. Payability loss is defined against the treatment-charge terms actually stipulated in signed contracts, compared with a fair reference. Penalty loss is defined as any deduction labelled a penalty without a stated, verifiable justification in the contract or the assay report. Unpriced-content loss covers secondary elements genuinely present in a concentrate, such as tantalum in a cassiterite lot or niobium in a coltan lot, that were not assayed or paid for. Prefinancing-interest loss is the interest actually paid on prefinancing arrangements, benchmarked against a reasonable cost of capital. This activity also proposes the historical period to be covered, since 'a given period' in the Board's request needs a number of years fixed before data collection can start.

A December Board deliverable.
