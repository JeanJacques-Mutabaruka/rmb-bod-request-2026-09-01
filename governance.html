Governance

Who does what, and how information reaches a decision

The mineral market intelligence function reports on two lines at once, and the same written products serve both. Offices are named rather than individuals, since post-holders change.

Responsibilities marked (P) are proposals requiring confirmation by Senior Management

Reporting lines

Two lines, one set of products

RMB Senior Management
The CEO, the CGO, the heads of department and the division managers, together with any other officer whose responsibilities in the sector make notification necessary.

Operational direction, resources, technical arbitration.

Board finance committee
An informal committee chaired by Mr. Dorian Cyubahiro, Advisor to the Minister of Finance and member of the RMB Board of Directors. It requires no formal constitution.

Strategic direction, validation of proposed mechanisms before implementation, financing matters.

A proposal reaches Senior Management first and the Board committee second, so the committee receives a technically reviewed document with a management position attached. The exception is an alert, which goes to the CEO and the committee chair at the same time, within 48 hours. Licence revocation is decided by the CEO on the recommendation of the competent RMB organ.
