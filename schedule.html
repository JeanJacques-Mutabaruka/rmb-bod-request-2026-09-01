Critical path

A1.2, the demonstration platform, is the first link. In parallel, A2.1 feeds A2.2 (trading company mapping), A3.1 and A3.2 feed A3.3 (the loss and gain estimate), A3.6 feeds A3.7 (the profit-and-reinvestment estimate), and A4.1 feeds A4.2 (the risk-pricing model). Any slippage before 15 November 2026 (D+75) threatens the loss and gain deliverable specifically; any slippage before 30 November 2026 (D+90) costs the Board meeting more broadly.
